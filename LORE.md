# Weirdport — UFO lore, our spin

Real UFO and paranormal folklore, reinterpreted as game systems. We don't follow the lore exactly;
we take what people already half-believe and give it a job in the game. Each entry: the lore, our
spin, and what it does in play.

## The orbs (the big one)

**The lore:** glowing spheres seen floating, hovering and darting, from WWII "foo fighters" to modern
sightings. Theories run from plasma, to drones, to surveillance probes, to a "control grid" over
the planet, to spirits and poltergeists.

**Our spin: the orbs are the evil aliens' control grid.** They hang over the city, link to each
other with faint beams, and each one does a job. A district under orbs is a district under alien
control, and the map shows the grid.

| Orb | What it does | Shoot it down and… |
|---|---|---|
| **Watcher** | sweeps a searchlight; seeing you raises the aliens' alert | the alert drops; it can't call others |
| **Pacifier** | people under it fall into a trance: walk in loops, stare, obey | they fall out of the trance, dazed: "where am I?" |
| **Amplifier** | stokes anger: protests get louder, rallies get angrier, brawls break out | the crowd calms down |
| **Projector** | throws holograms: fake walls, fake roadblocks, fake buildings, fake people | the illusion flickers and vanishes, opening the way |
| **Poltergeist** | haunts a building: throws furniture, flickers lights, slams doors | the haunting stops |
| **Relay** | links others into the grid and powers them | every orb it fed weakens or drops |

Orbs are small, high and fast, which makes them a good use for the blaster and for flying forms.
Taking a district's grid apart is how you start curing it.

## Electromagnetic effects: the tell

**The lore:** cars stall, lights flicker, radios crackle and compasses spin near a UFO.

**Our spin:** the same happens near orbs and alien activity. Streetlights flicker, cars stall in the
road, radios hiss and screens glitch. It's atmosphere and a hint: you can hear and see where the
aliens are before you find them.

## Crop circles and cattle (the farm, Chapter 0)

**The lore:** overnight patterns flattened into fields; cows found strangely mutilated.

**Our spin:** crop circles are landing beacons and messages, readable from above, and some are
puzzles. Cattle are what the aliens are harvesting. Gristle Farm's opening can show both: a circle
in the wheat, and a cow lifted up in a beam on the night you crash.

## Abduction and tractor beams

**The lore:** beams of light lifting people into craft.

**Our spin:** traversal and failure. Enemy beams lift civilians (rescue them). Some beams can be
hijacked to ride up onto rooftops. Getting caught in one yourself leads to missing time (below).

## Missing time

**The lore:** abductees report hours they can't account for.

**Our spin:** a fail state that isn't death. Get captured and the screen whites out; you wake
somewhere else, minutes later, with juice drained and Clancy looking for you.

## Men in Black

**The lore:** strangely dressed agents who visit witnesses and make them forget.

**Our spin, two sides:** the hidden good aliens' agents can wipe witnesses, which is a way to lose
heat after people saw you. The evil aliens have their own version, wiping people who saw too much.

## Implants

**The lore:** tiny devices abductees say were put in their bodies.

**Our spin:** some civilians carry implants that let the aliens see through their eyes or steer
them. Clancy can sniff out who has one, and removing it frees them.

## Shapeshifters and reptilians

**The lore:** beings who wear human form and secretly run things.

**Our spin:** this is the Skinsuits already — aliens wearing people, rising through the ranks to the
top of Dump Tower. They're the root of the "they're secretly running everything" joke both sides of
the culture war tell about each other.

## Holograms and fake skies

**The lore:** "Project Blue Beam": projected fake events in the sky.

**Our spin:** projector orbs at city scale. A fake skyline, a fake event to rile up a crowd, a fake
sun to hide the Mothership. Cutting the power shows what's really there.

## Black triangles and tic-tacs

**The lore:** silent black triangular craft at night; white capsule-shaped craft that out-fly jets.

**Our spin:** enemy craft. Triangles patrol at night and drop orbs. Tic-tacs are interceptors that
chase you when you're gliding or flying.

## Poltergeists and high strangeness

**The lore:** ranches and houses where objects move, lights appear and animals panic.

**Our spin:** haunted buildings are the poltergeist orb's work. Animals react first: cows stampede
and dogs bark. Clancy's ears go up when something is near.

## The Hum

**The lore:** a low drone some people say they hear everywhere.

**Our spin:** an ambient audio layer that gets louder where the grid is strong and drops when you
free a district. You can hear a district get better.
